## About the Project

Resume Matcher helps you create a comprehensive master resume that you can use to tailor for each job application.

### How It Works

1. **Upload** your master resume (PDF or DOCX)
2. **Paste** a job description you're targeting
3. **Review** AI-generated improvements and tailored content
4. **Cover Letter** generator for the job application
5. **Customize** the layout and sections to fit your style
6. **Export** as a professional PDF with your preferred template

## Key Features

* **Resume Builder**: AI-powered tailoring for specific roles.
* **Cover Letter Generator**: Create tailored cover letters based on the job description.
* **Resume Scoring & Keyword Highlighting**: Analyze your resume against the job description with match scores and improvement suggestions.
* **PDF Export**: Export your tailored resume and cover letter in professional templates.
* **Internationalization**: Multi-language UI and content support.

## Problem Statement
Students and job seekers often struggle to understand whether their resumes align with job requirements. Manual screening is time-consuming and may overlook important skill gaps. There is a need for an intelligent system that can automatically analyze resumes and match them with relevant job descriptions.

## Project Description
Resume Skill Matcher is an AI-based application that compares resumes with job descriptions to identify matching skills, calculate a compatibility score, and highlight missing skills. It helps users improve their resumes and better prepare for placement and recruitment opportunities.
